In August of 2016, OneLogin was growing at a rapid pace by increasing revenue, hiring more staff, and offering new services. OneLogin has always innovated at lightning speed, but we were hitting a bottleneck with service configuration, monitoring setup, instance provisioning, and operational backlogs. This meant getting new services to production would take months after they were ready to ship.
