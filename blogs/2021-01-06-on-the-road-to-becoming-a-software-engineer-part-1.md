“Why does a recruiter have a bunch of commits in the company’s git repository?” This question has come up a lot since I started working on a React app with Dominick Caponi, an engineer on the OneLogin Developer Experience team (DevEx). I am a recruiter at OneLogin, but I am also learning to become a software engineer. The best way to learn is to apply what you learned from books or from a bootcamp to something in the real world.
